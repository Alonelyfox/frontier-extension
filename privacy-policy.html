Privacy Policy — Frontier Ledger

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Frontier Ledger is a browser extension that lets a community record crafting recipes and calculate the resources needed to make things. This policy explains what information the extension handles and how.

1. What information is collected

The extension stores only the content you and other users of the extension enter into it:

  • The names and quantities of "goods" and resources you record
  • Any image URLs you optionally attach to a resource (these are links you paste; the images themselves stay hosted wherever you linked them, not on our servers)

The extension does not ask for or collect your name, email address, physical location, browsing history, or any other personally identifying information.

2. Anonymous authentication

To keep the shared data safe from random internet users, the extension signs each installation in anonymously using Firebase Authentication. This creates a random identifier with no personal information attached to it — it is not tied to a Google account, email, or name, and it exists only to allow read/write access to the shared database.

3. Where information is stored

Recipe data is stored in a Firebase Realtime Database, a cloud service operated by Google. That means Google's own privacy and security practices also apply to how the data is transmitted and stored on their infrastructure. You can read Google's policies at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Who can see this information

This extension uses a single shared database: anyone who has the extension installed can view, add, edit, or delete any entry. There are no individual accounts, private entries, or per-person permissions. Do not enter anything you wouldn't want visible to everyone using the extension.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking scripts.
  • We don't sell or share data with third parties for advertising or marketing.
  • We don't collect data beyond what's described above.

6. Third-party image links

If you or another user pastes a link to an image (for example, hosted on Discord, Imgur, or elsewhere), loading that thumbnail means your browser makes a request to that third-party host. That host's own privacy practices apply to that request — this extension has no control over, and does not vet, links entered by users.

7. Data retention and removal

Recorded entries persist in the shared database until a user of the extension deletes them ("Strike out" in the ledger), or until whoever administers the underlying Firebase project removes them or deletes the project entirely.

8. Children's privacy

This extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the extension after a change means you accept the updated policy.
