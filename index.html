FRONTIER LEDGER

A Reckoning of Goods & Supplies
✦

What is this?

Frontier Ledger is a browser extension built for a small roleplay community to record crafting recipes and work out exactly what's needed to make more of anything — down to the raw materials, no matter how many steps of crafting stand in between.

  • Record what any item takes to craft, including how much a single craft produces
  • Chain recipes together — a good can call on another recorded good as one of its own supplies
  • Reckon up several different goods at once and get one combined shopping list of raw materials
  • Shared in real time with everyone else in the community using the extension

Policies

These apply to anyone using the Frontier Ledger extension.
